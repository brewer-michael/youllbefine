✅ Your Checklist

1
File the FAFSA
This is step zero. It unlocks federal loans, the PA State Grant, and work-study. Takes about 30 minutes.
studentaid.gov
2
Apply for the PA State Grant
Free money — up to ~$5,750/year. Complete it in the PA State Grant Form after your FAFSA.
pheaa.org
3
Ask LBC about institutional scholarships
Most LBC students get aid. Ask the financial aid office directly: "What else can I qualify for?"
LBC Financial Aid Office
4
Borrow federal before private
Federal loans have lower fixed rates, income-driven repayment, and forgiveness options private loans simply don't.
5
Keep your grades up
Most scholarships require a 3.0+ GPA. It's the single biggest financial lever you control.
6
Teacher forgiveness is real
Teach 5 years at a qualifying school: up to $17,500 of federal loans gone. Public-school teachers may also qualify for PSLF (full forgiveness after 10 years).
Keep teaching and filing the paperwork.
